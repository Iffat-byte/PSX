Privacy

PSX Personal Assistant helps you keep a record of your Pakistan Stock Exchange shares. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you enter.

In short: this is a private website for one person. It opens only with a secret link. We never sell data and never ask for a broker password.

What you enter

  • Your trades, dividends, cash entries, prices and reminders.
  • Your settings, such as language, broker charge rates and tax rate.

Where it is kept

  • In a database run by Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India). The database answers only to the secret link; without it nothing can be read or changed.
  • A copy is kept in your browser so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • Anyone who has the link can open the website, so don't share it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't ask for, store or use your broker login, CNIC or bank details.
  • We don't place trades or move money. The app only keeps accounts.

Services the page uses

  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, and public code libraries are loaded from jsDelivr and cdnjs.
  • Supabase stores the data.
  • EODHD supplies daily closing prices. Only share symbols are sent to them, never your quantities or money.
  • Screenshots you upload for prices are read on your own device and are not sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can erase your entries in Settings. To remove everything for good, ask the site owner and the data will be deleted.

Not investment advice

The app is for record-keeping and learning. Prices marked "demo" and announcements marked "sample" are not real PSX data. Check important figures with your broker and consult a licensed adviser before investing.
